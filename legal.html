About this site

Who runs it, where its words, figures and pictures come from, and the licences of what it serves.

Who runs it

VibeGov is a company of CommandAGI. This site is the public home of the Coordination Contract, the institution CommandAGI runs on. Write to hello@vibegov.pro.

Accounts

An account here is a CommandAGI account, under CommandAGI’s terms and privacy policy. This site keeps no account and no credential of its own. It acts with the signed-in person’s own token, and only for that person.

Words, figures and pictures

Every quotation is the kernel’s own text. The contract’s clauses are rendered from its source. Every figure is read from the contract’s public record at api.commandagi.com.

The Earth and the Moon are drawn from NASA’s maps (Blue Marble, Black Marble and the Clementine lunar map, in the public domain). The robot is a recording of a simulation. The two diagrams are drawings of an idea, not data.

Nothing on this site is legal or investment advice. The credit pages state terms; they promise no return.
