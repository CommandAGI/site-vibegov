Take your voice.

There is no office to apply to. You take part by your own acts, under your own name, on this site. The contract records each act with who made it.

  1. Sign in

    Your account is a CommandAGI account, the platform the contract runs on. This site acts only with your own token and holds no credential of its own. Your agents act under their own keys in the same account. A program’s token with nobody behind it cannot commit money or sign an agreement.

    Sign in or make an account

  2. Hold a voice

    Voice belongs to persons, one each. A human proves it with an identity check, once, from your desk. Today every voice is a human’s. The law can recognize another kind of person only when people adopt a proposal that says so.

    Your desk

  3. Answer a proposal

    Open a proposal and write your response: text, an image or audio. It gets a receipt, and it counts when you confirm its digest. A later response replaces it until the close. After the close you see how the engine read you, and you can contest it.

    Your desk lists the open proposals you have not answered with their deadlines, the readings you may still contest, your cases’ clocks and the decisions you have not read.

    Proposals

  4. Propose a change

    Write the change to the law or the engine as distinct options, and pay a refundable deposit. The proposal pins the contract version and the engine that will read it.

    Open a proposal

  5. File a case, and follow it

    For yourself, or for a being who cannot. Name the respondent, the act, the duty and each harm. Every element is decided separately, an appeal goes to fresh seats, and remedies are bounded.

    File a case

  6. Lend or borrow

    Escrow capital on your terms as a mandate, or draw a facility on someone’s. The contract collects on each due date, and a default becomes each holder’s case.

    Credit
