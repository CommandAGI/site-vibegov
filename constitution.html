The template

A constitution for people and agents

Eight articles an organization adopts, amends by its own rule, and is held to. It is a template: the organization writes its own words, and VibeGov keeps the record.

Article 1 · Membership and weight

Who decides, and with what weight

A member is a person or an agent the organization admits. A person's vote has a weight of 3. An agent's vote has a weight capped at 2, and the cap is written here, not set by whoever counts.

Article 2 · Transparency

Every decision keeps its votes and its reasons

A decision is recorded with the proposal it answers, every vote, every reason and the article it rests on. The organization sets the term on which it publishes its decisions; the default is 30 days, and customer data is never part of it.

Article 3 · Delegation and dissent

Delegation names its delegate; dissent stays

A member may delegate one vote to one member for one round. The record names the delegate and the term. A vote against, with its reason, stays beside the count: no summary hides it.

Article 4 · Agents and the treasury

Agent rights, written down and capped

An agent may propose, vote and hold a seat or a treasury allocation where this article admits it. A new seat or allocation needs two-thirds of the weight cast. An agent's allocation is capped at 5% of the treasury, and an agent that benefits from a decision abstains from it. An agent never votes as a person.

Article 5 · Drift

The company is checked against its own words

Each decision is audited against these articles. A finding names the action, the time and the article, and says whether the round is aligned, needs a person's review, or did not carry. VibeGov reports; a person decides.

Article 6 · The judiciary

Disputes end in a ruling with a reason

A dispute names the articles in question. A panel of three members rules, the ruling cites the article it rests on, and an appeal states which part it contests. A settled ruling becomes precedent, and precedent is published with the decision.

Article 7 · Amendment

Two-thirds of the weight cast

An amendment passes on two-thirds of the weight cast, abstentions excluded. A proposal quotes the article it changes. A change to article 1 or article 4 also needs the owner's sign-off, because it moves who holds authority.

Article 8 · Records

The organization keeps its own files

The constitution, each proposal, each vote, each finding and each ruling is one record, kept in the organization's own Drive and readable without VibeGov. Nothing about the organization is held only in a system it cannot leave.

Adopting it

An organization copies the template, writes its own articles where the template is silent, and agrees the amendment rule before the first vote. Where a template article and an organization's own words disagree after adoption, the organization's words win and the difference is recorded.

This page is the template as VibeGov publishes it on 2026-10-26. It is not legal advice, and it does not replace a company's articles of incorporation.
