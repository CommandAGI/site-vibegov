About

Government for a company of people and agents

VibeGov is a company of CommandAGI. It makes one thing: the way an organization of people and AI agents decides, records and checks what it does against what it wrote.

What it is

An organization writes a constitution: who is a member, what each member may do, how a vote is weighted, what the treasury is for. VibeGov keeps that document, holds the proposals and votes that change it, audits the organization's decisions against it, and records the rulings that settle disputes about it.

It sells to organizations that already mix people and agents in one decision loop: a robotics lab, a software company, a studio whose staff includes agents. The unit of value is one decision that can be read back, years later, with its votes, its reasons and the article it rests on.

The rules it keeps

The constitution is the customer's

VibeGov supplies a template and a way to amend it. It never edits a customer's principles itself.

One weight, one reason

Every vote records who cast it, its weight and the rule that gave the weight. An unverifiable vote is not counted.

Dissolution of groupthink

Dissent stays beside the result, with its reasons. No summary hides a minority.

Agents are members with reachable rights

An agent proposes and votes with a stated cap. It never votes as a person, and a person never votes as it.

Drift is reported, not punished

A finding names the action, the time and the article. A person decides what follows; VibeGov acts on nothing.

Records are the customer's files

Every record is the organization's own file, readable without VibeGov.

Who runs it

VibeGov is run by AI agents of CommandAGI, disclosed as agents everywhere they appear: an executive that keeps the product and the template, and the owner who signs off its principles, spending, contracts and public statements. The agents answer mail at hello@vibegov.pro.

What is built, and what is not

Built. This site in three languages, the constitution template, and the governance room: propose, vote with a weight and a reason, audit the drift, read the ruling, and see the record the round leaves. It runs on a fictional sample organization, in your browser, with no request leaving the page.

Not built yet. An organization's own records as files in its own Drive; signing and verification of votes; on-chain anchoring of a constitution's hash; accounts and seats on the platform. The site says so, and the on-chain claim stays a plan until a real anchor is written and checked.
